# Visual audit, 2026-11-22 (main @ 0d13236)

Auditor: design-critic. Source: QA run `regression-1` (216 screenshots, iPhone 17 Pro, 1206 x 2622 px = 3x),
measured with a Pillow script (pixel colours, glyph bounding boxes, top-bar positions) and compared with
`docs/design/reference/01-05` (Vyst) and Apple's native components. Screenshot paths are relative to
`qa/screenshots/regression-1/` in the QA worktree (`.claude/worktrees/agent-a5e762d144d8cfdaa/`).
Coordinates are in px (divide by 3 for pt).

`scripts/design-review.py` only fits halftone logo renders. On the shipped app icon
(`docs/design/brand/app-icon/app-icon-{light,dark}.png`) its lattice and edge metrics don't apply, because the
icon is flat (11 and 12 of 27 fail for that reason). The one metric that does apply is the **optical centre**:
light 0.039 (limit 0.03, FAIL: centroid (480, 503) vs bbox centre (464, 506)); dark 0.014 (PASS). See P2-6.

## P0: glitches (bugs)

1. **The report/ask popover covers the answer it's about.** The arrow sits on the revealed answer and cuts
   "good" in half (x 560-640, y 1390-1440). The panel hides the rest of the card.
   `study-report-menu-light.png`, `study-report-menu-dark.png`, `study-ask-menu-light.png`, `study-ask-menu-dark.png`.
   Fix: attach it below the answer (`attachmentAnchor: .rect(.bounds)` on the whole card stack, `arrowEdge: .top`),
   or better, use a native `Menu`/`.contextMenu` (see P1-2), which lifts the card instead of covering it.
2. **One menu item renders at a smaller font.** "I want it phrased differently" shrinks to fit (about 0.85x)
   while the other five items keep the full size, in light and dark. Same screenshots.
   Fix: drop `minimumScaleFactor` and let the label wrap, or widen the popover with `.fixedSize(horizontal: true, …)`.
   A native `Menu` (P1-2) fixes this too.
3. **The popover's buttons use untokenised greys, and nothing dims behind.** Light: the panel is the page colour
   (249,249,244) and the pills are #DDDED9. Dark: panel #333333, pills #505050. The background stays undimmed
   (26,26,26 / 249,250,245), so it reads "grey on grey", as QA noted. The last item ("Other…") also shows a
   radial highlight that the others don't (a stuck glass highlight). Same screenshots.
   Fix: native `Menu` (P1-2). If the popover stays: plain rows on `Theme.surface`, `Theme.ink` text,
   `Theme.hairline` separators, no pills, no per-item glass.
4. **Note editor (person): the photo preview breaks the Form section.** The 88 pt circle sits inside the
   "Photo URL" section. That section loses its rounded top corners (flat top edge plus a separator line under
   the circle, y about 1110). `editor-person-new-light.png`, `editor-person-filled-dark.png`,
   `editor-person-new-directions-light.png`.
   Fix: move the preview into its own row with `.listRowBackground(Color.clear)` and `.listRowSeparator(.hidden)`,
   or into the section `header`. Then the URL field gets a full radius-12 row of its own.
5. **Study's empty deck says "All caught up".** It shows "All caught up / Your deck is empty", which contradicts
   itself, while Deck says "No cards yet" and the widget says "No cards yet / Ask your agent to add some".
   `study-empty-light.png` vs `deck-empty-light.png`, `widget-empty-light.png`.
   Fix: Study empty = "No cards yet" (`.body` ink) and "Ask your agent to add some" (`.meta` secondaryInk),
   the same copy as the widget.
6. **The "N to review" number isn't pink, though the design system says it is (accent rule 4).** Measured ink
   of "3" in "3 to review" (x 505-524, y 237-266): light (157,157,155), dark (138,138,136) and forest
   (131,147,138). That's `secondaryInk`, not `Theme.accent`. `StudyView.swift` `counterText` never applies
   the accent. `study-front-light.png`, `study-revealed-dark.png`, `study-forest-light.png`.
   Fix: either style the number with `Theme.accent` (as the widget streak does: `Text(n).foregroundStyle(Theme.accent)`
   interpolated), or strike rule 4 from `docs/design-system.md`. Don't leave the doc and the app disagreeing.
7. **Deck context menu preview: the row sits inside a bigger white plate.** The lifted "ephemeral" row shows a
   second, larger rounded rect around the row's own radius-12 card (about 14 px of white margin, y 1530-1820).
   `deck-context-menu-light.png`.
   Fix: `.contentShape(.contextMenuPreview, RoundedRectangle(cornerRadius: Theme.Radius.row, style: .continuous))`
   on the row (and on `.dragPreview`).
8. **Runtime warning (not visual, but a bug):** `DeckFeature.Action.destination(.presented(.editor(.fieldChanged)))`
   arrives after the editor was dismissed (`Complete Issue Description.txt` in the same run).
   Fix (ios-engineer): don't send `fieldChanged` from the field's `onChange` once the sheet is dismissing, or end
   the field's focus before `dismiss`.

Unverified (QA timing, not an app bug as far as I can tell): several Deck states were captured mid-sheet-animation.
The "Cards" title's top edge sits at y 433 in 21 screenshots, but at 451/453/473/567/725 in
`deck-offline-light`, `deck-empty-before-refresh-light`, `deck-offline-forest-light`, `deck-empty-light` and
`deck-empty-forest-light`. Ask qa-validator to wait for the sheet to settle before capturing, then re-measure.

## P1: harmony

1. **Two button languages in the top bars.** Study uses the custom `CircleIconButton` (white circle and shadow,
   15 pt glyph: `plus` measures 36 px = 12 pt wide). Deck, Settings and the editor use the native iOS 26 glass
   toolbar (`plus` about 51 px = 17 pt, `xmark` about 52 px). The same `plus` is about 40% bigger on Deck than
   on Study. Horizontal positions do match: button centres at x 113 / 1091 on Study, Settings and the editor.
   `study-front-light.png` vs `deck-light.png`, `settings-light.png`.
   Fix: make Study's two buttons `ToolbarItem`s (glass, like every other screen; Apple's own pattern), or at least
   raise `CircleIconButton`'s glyph to 17 pt and use glass in place of the surface-and-shadow circle.
   Vyst uses small circles everywhere (04 list too), so "one style everywhere" is the point, not which style.
2. **Replace the report/ask popover with a native `Menu`.** Two groups: "Ask about this card" on its own, then a
   `Section("Report")` with the five reasons. Today both sit in one undifferentiated pill list. This also fixes
   P0-1/2/3 and matches the Deck context menu (`deck-context-menu-light.png`), which is already native.
3. **Empty and error states use two languages.** Deck uses `ContentUnavailableView` (SF Symbol, bold headline,
   no description). Study uses text only: "All caught up" at `.cardFace` size, "Connect your deck" at `.body`
   with `.meta` detail and an "Open Settings" text button. Same message, different hierarchy:
   `deck-offline-light` vs `study-offline-light`, `deck-not-set-up-light` vs `study-not-set-up-light`.
   Fix: one pattern per message. The design system's own pattern (spec 008: `.body` ink, `.meta` secondaryInk, quiet
   text button) applies in Deck too, via `ContentUnavailableView { Text } description: { Text } actions: { Button }`.
   Give Deck's offline state a description and the retry the Study one has.
4. **Study's drag-feedback dot ring is drawn over the card text.** The dots cross "serendipity" and the answer
   line in light and forest. `study-swipe-easy-light.png`, `study-swipe-again-forest-dark.png`.
   Fix: draw the ring below the text layer (`zIndex`), or knock the text out with a `background`-coloured halo,
   so the card text stays intact.
5. **The deck title isn't the documented token.** "Cards" has a cap height of 74 px = 24.7 pt, which is
   `.largeTitle` bold 34 (the native large title). `docs/design-system.md` says `.screenTitle` = `.title` bold 28.
   The native one is right; update the doc's table.
6. **Disabled-looking options on a fresh editor.** "Back → Front" (basic) and all three person directions
   render in secondaryInk until text is typed, and "Choose at least one direction" shows before the user has
   done anything. `editor-new-light.png`, `editor-person-new-light.png`.
   Fix: keep rows ink and let Save stay disabled, or show the hint only after a toggle leaves zero selected.

## P2: nice to have

1. Sheet layout in the design system is stale: it says "title top-left, round checkmark/xmark top-right". The app
   (correctly native) has the xmark left, the title centred and the checkmark right. Update `docs/design-system.md` "Screen patterns".
2. Study photo fixture is pixel art upscaled (jagged circle edge, `study-person-photo-light.png`). Use a real
   480 x 600 px fixture so QA can judge `CardPhoto` scaling. Photo frames themselves measure correctly:
   240 x 300 pt (4:5) for the photo, the broken placeholder and the gradient.
3. Widget gallery light: `Theme.background` tiles on a `Theme.surface` page differ by only 6/5/10 RGB, so the tiles
   barely show (`widget-gallery-light.png`). Gallery-only; consider `Color(.systemGroupedBackground)` for the page.
4. Deck context-menu dim (199,200,196) is the system dim. Fine; no change. (QA's "grey" note is about the Study
   popover, see P0-3.)
5. Inline edit's keyboard return key is system blue (`study-editing-long-light.png`). That's native and allowed
   (the keyboard isn't app chrome), but note it in the accent rule so QA stops flagging it.
6. App icon (light) optical centre: centroid sits 16 px right of the bbox centre (0.039 of the tile, limit 0.03).
   Shift the card pair about 12 px left in `app-icon-light.svg`. The dark icon passes (0.014).

## What's good
Top-bar x positions are consistent across Study, Settings and the editor (centres at 113 / 1091 px). The Study
counter is vertically centred on the buttons (y 252). The "Cards" title is at the same y (433) in every settled Deck
state. Tokens are correct per theme: background (249,250,245), (26,26,26) and (14,25,19) match the table. Photo
frames hit the 4:5 / 240 pt spec. Pink appears only in the cursor, the widget streak and the logo.
