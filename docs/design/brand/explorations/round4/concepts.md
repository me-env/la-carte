# Round 4: knowledge consolidation concepts

How memory science and SRS apps describe it: each retrieval **strengthens a trace** (synaptic potentiation), the
**forgetting curve** decays more slowly after every review (FSRS "stability" grows), intervals **expand**
(1 d → 3 d → 8 d …), and knowledge moves from fragile to stable, which people picture as layers settling, a path
being worn in, a line being re-traced until it's bold. Vyst's lesson: one gesture, flat white on warm near-black,
and the mark *is* the product mechanic.
