# Logo: decision (2026-11-22)

The user chose **round 11, accent option 1 on mark 131**: a portrait card crossed by the forgetting curve as one
line, the line cut out inside the card, the line outside the card in the brand pink.

- Source geometry: `explorations/ideation-gen/forgetcurve.py` variant 131, treatments in
  `explorations/ideation-gen/accent131.py` (option 1 = "line outside the card in pink").
- Production: `render.py` freezes that geometry and writes the logo SVG/PNGs, the app icons and
  `ios/LaCarteKit/Sources/DesignSystem/LaCarteLogoGeometry.swift`.
- The critic noted option 1 uses more pink than the old "very small amounts" rule. The user chose it, so the brand
  rule now names the logo's line as the one larger pink use (`../brand.md`, Palette).

The earlier briefs (the fanned two-card mark, the halftone rounds 12–15) are history in `explorations/`.
